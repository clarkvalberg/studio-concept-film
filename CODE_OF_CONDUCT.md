# Code of Conduct

This project follows a simple principle: **be a thoughtful, honest, generous contributor**, and treat others as you'd want to be treated in a room of people doing serious work.

We adopt the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as our baseline.

## In practice

**Welcome:**

- Pushback on opinions in this repo — that's what makes the work better
- Disagreement, expressed directly and specifically
- Questions, including questions that seem basic
- Contributions from people with no prior experience making concept films

**Not welcome:**

- Personal attacks or harassment of any kind
- Discrimination on any basis
- Bad-faith engagement, trolling, or argumentation in service of nothing
- Aggressive enforcement of stylistic preferences as if they were ethical violations
- Sustained derailment of conversations from the substantive work

## Enforcement

Issues are handled by the maintainers. If you experience or witness behavior that violates this code, please reach out privately to one of the maintainers listed in [README.md](README.md). Reports will be handled in confidence.

The maintainers reserve the right to take any action they consider appropriate — including removing comments, closing issues, or banning contributors — for behavior they deem violative of this code or detrimental to the project's health.
